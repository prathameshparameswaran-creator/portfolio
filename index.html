Case study

How Precize's Instagram grew from 18K to 24.8K followers in four months, Jun to Oct 2026. A team effort; this is the plan I worked on.

The challenge

Precize sells unlisted and pre-IPO shares, a topic most retail investors find complex. The content had to explain it simply and still be fun enough to follow every week.

The goal

Grow an engaged Instagram audience, build a direct channel to followers, and make pre-IPO investing feel simple and relatable.

01Consistency

A fixed content week

Precize Times on Monday, product videos on Wednesday, Market Pulse on Friday, memes on Saturday. Followers knew what was coming.

02Reach

Memes and reels for discovery

Relatable memes and short reels were made to be shared and reach new people; explainers and Market Pulse gave them a reason to stay.

03Community

Comments into conversations

Comment-to-DM follow-ups turned engagement into one-on-one chats and helped grow the broadcast channel from 0 to 500 members in three months.

04Borrowed trust

Finance creator collabs

I pitched creators and closed 8. The FinSingh skit reached 109,463 views with 2.1K shares and 1.8K saves.
